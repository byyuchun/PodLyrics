# Waveform Offset uses a downloaded Local Episode Asset

The playhead from MediaRemote is the timeline. Waveform Offset is only a short correction around that playhead, never its own clock — otherwise a seek stays stuck on the old lock. Search is always centered on the current elapsed time; a Panel Highlight jump that the playhead does not yet know is followed until MediaRemote catches up. Stream Cache is ignored. Screen Recording is required to capture the playing audio.
